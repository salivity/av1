MediaTek Dimensity Chipsets with AV1 Decoding

MediaTek played a pioneering role in mobile video streaming by being the first chipmaker to integrate hardware-based AV1 video decoding into smartphone processors. This article details the specific MediaTek Dimensity chipsets that introduced AV1 hardware decoding to mobile devices, highlighting how the Dimensity 1000 series led the transition toward more bandwidth-efficient video playback.

The Dimensity 1000: The World's First AV1 Mobile SoC

Announced in late 2019, the flagship MediaTek Dimensity 1000 was the world’s first mobile system-on-chip (SoC) to feature an integrated hardware AV1 decoder. The chipset was capable of handling AV1 video streams at up to 4K resolution at 60 frames per second.

Following the standard Dimensity 1000, MediaTek expanded the technology across variants in the same family:

  • MediaTek Dimensity 1000L: A slightly lower-clocked variant designed for mid-to-high tier devices that retained native AV1 decoding.
  • MediaTek Dimensity 1000+: An upgraded flagship refresh released in mid-2020 that continued support for 4K AV1 hardware playback while offering higher display refresh rates.

Subsequent Dimensity Chipsets with AV1 Support

After establishing the standard with the 1000 series, MediaTek continued to include dedicated AV1 decoding hardware in subsequent generations:

  • Dimensity 1100 and Dimensity 1200: Released in early 2021, these 6nm premium SoCs maintained hardware-level decoding for AV1 media streams.
  • Dimensity 9000 Series: MediaTek’s ultra-flagship tiers, beginning with the Dimensity 9000 in late 2021, expanded decoding capabilities further, handling high-bitrate 4K and 8K AV1 playback.

The Impact of Early AV1 Mobile Hardware

Prior to the Dimensity 1000, mobile devices relied on software decoding for AV1 media, which caused high CPU usage, thermal throttling, and rapid battery depletion. The AV1 codec provides roughly 30% better compression efficiency compared to H.265 (HEVC) and VP9 without sacrificing visual fidelity. By integrating a dedicated hardware decoding block, MediaTek enabled smartphones to stream high-resolution content from platforms like YouTube with significantly lower power consumption and reduced mobile data usage.
